# M161: Data whose scores do not vary within any subject are refused classed before any fit

**Status:** done (2026-10-17, PR #180 https://github.com/jmgirard/intraclass/pull/180)

**Goal:** `icc()` refuses data in which no subject's scores vary with a classed `intraclass_singular_fit` condition, raised before any model is fitted, on every engine.

**Outcome:** An input check in `icc()` (`R/icc.R`, just before the engine-fit dispatch) aborts with `intraclass_singular_fit` when at least one subject has two or more non-missing scores and no subject has two different scores. The comparison is exact (D-052, extending D-022). It runs after the argument fences and most design fences. The lavaan replicate fence and the CI-function fences come later and are pre-empted on such data. The message names `score` and says that flat ratings mean perfect agreement within every subject. Before, such data ended in unclassed glmmTMB or lme4 errors, or in a one-way lme4 ICC 1 [1, 1]. New `tests/testthat/test-flat-scores.R` runs a grid of engine × model × `ci_method` on six flat frames and their controls, with stubbed engine fits, to show that the refusal comes before any fit. `?icc`, NEWS and the DESIGN.md Boundary-fit policy record it. Absorbed candidate row "Data with no variation within subjects ends in an unclassed glmmTMB error".

**Decisions:** D-052 (plan commit). Plan-gate alternatives were a per-model residual check and a new condition class. Both are recorded in the work log in git.
