# M166: d_study() bands from glmmTMB and lme4 fits are refused or warned on by each curve's single-rating coefficient

**Status:** done (2026-10-19, PR #185 https://github.com/jmgirard/intraclass/pull/185)

**Goal:** On a glmmTMB or lme4 fit, `d_study()` reads each projected curve's single-rating coefficient from the band's own draws, refuses the band when that coefficient's limits are exactly equal, and warns when its lower limit lies within 1e-12 of 1.

**Outcome:** `d_study()` builds, on glmmTMB and lme4 fits only, one single-rating estimand per distinct type and level of the band (`make_estimand()` at `m = 1`, occasion 1 on replicate fits) and takes its interval from the band's Monte-Carlo components or stored bootstrap resamples. `check_band_single_rating()` (R/d-study.R) calls `refuse_zero_width()`, whose hint names the coefficients with equal limits through the shared `zero_width()`, then the new `warn_unresolved_band()` (R/ci-montecarlo.R), which reads the same `near_one()` test as `warn_unresolved_interval()` with d = 1.
