# M164: The near-1 interval warning reads each row against 1e-12 divided by the ratings it averages, and names its rows

**Status:** done (2026-10-18, PR #183 https://github.com/jmgirard/intraclass/pull/183)

**Goal:** On glmmTMB and lme4 Monte-Carlo and bootstrap intervals, the near-1 warning reads a row that averages d ratings against the bound 1e-12/d.

**Outcome:** `warn_unresolved_interval(intervals, estimands, engine)` (`R/ci-montecarlo.R`) reads each interval against `unresolved_interval_bound / d`, d the largest error divisor of that interval's own estimand (raters averaged, times occasions where the row averages occasions). It compares the gap `1 - low` with the bound, since `1 - bound/d` rounds near 1 from d of about 4500; above d of about 9000 only equal limits reach it, and they abort first. The message names each firing row through `unresolved_row_name()` (label, level, occasion count) joined by `collapse_and()` without cli's 20-name cut, scopes the 1e-12 sentence to single-rating rows, and adds the projected-count sentence only for a numeric `unit` row. `mc_ci()` and `bootstrap_ci()` pass their estimands. Probe: on 20 subjects, 3 raters, noise SD 1e-6, single rows lie 1.64e-12 to 2.07e-12 and average rows 5.5e-13 to 6.9e-13 from 1, and no call warns now. `tests/testthat/test-unresolved-interval.R` (19 tests): stub bands for d = 3 and divisors 3, 3, 6 built from `icc_estimand()`, a mixed-d call, identity against a no-op helper in every case, shared-label name pairs, 25 rows, d = 8000, and `icc()` grids on M163's frames plus a noise-1e-6 frame and a 2-occasion replicate frame. NEWS entry and `?icc` (`ci_method`) state the scaled bound; DESIGN.md Monte-Carlo and Bootstrap rows cite D-056. `data-raw/record-claims.tsv` roadmap-terminal-rows expectation corrected (red on main since the M163 done pass).

**Decisions:** D-056 (plan commit): bound divided by the largest error divisor, rows named; supersedes D-055's decision and narrows D-054's rule to single-rating rows. The plan chose the largest divisor over the rater divisor and over recomputing single-rating coefficients; falsifiers are in the work log in git.
