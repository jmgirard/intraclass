# M162: Raw glmmTMB and lme4 errors reach the caller as intraclass_singular_fit, and lme4 refuses equal interval limits

**Status:** done (2026-10-17, PR #181 https://github.com/jmgirard/intraclass/pull/181)

**Goal:** On the glmmTMB and lme4 engines, a fit that cannot support an interval ends in the package's `intraclass_singular_fit` condition, never in an engine error without a package class.

**Outcome:** `reraise_engine_error()` (`R/abort.R`) wraps the `engine_fit` dispatch in `icc()` as a calling handler. On the glmmTMB and lme4 engines, an error that is not an rlang error is re-raised as `intraclass_singular_fit`, with the original error as its parent. That covers the fit and the covariance read after it. Package and rlang errors pass unchanged, and lavaan and brms are outside it. `refuse_zero_width()` (`R/ci-montecarlo.R`) now refuses exactly equal Monte-Carlo and bootstrap limits on lme4 fits as on glmmTMB, with no width bound. New `tests/testthat/test-zero-residual.R` runs 72 calls on zero-residual two-way data: on main, 28 ended in raw errors ("Downdated VtV is not positive definite", "'a' is computationally singular", LU factorization). It also forces engine errors through stubs, with pass-through controls. The equal-limit tests in `test-glmmtmb-false-zero.R` now cover lme4, with 1e-10-wide and lavaan controls. NEWS, `?icc` (`score`) and the DESIGN.md Boundary-fit policy rows record it. lme4 intervals printed [1, 1] on near-flat or exactly additive data have limits about 1e-14 apart and stay reported, as the user chose at a plan-time stop. Rows "two-way data with zero residual" and "M161 review follow-ups on the flat-scores refusal" were narrowed to that remainder.

**Decisions:** D-053 (plan commit). The plan gate chose the engine-side re-raise over an input-side refusal, one handler at the dispatch over handlers at each fit call, and `intraclass_singular_fit` over `intraclass_engine_error`. Each choice and its falsifier is in the work log in git.
