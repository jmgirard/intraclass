# M163: glmmTMB and lme4 intervals whose lower limit lies within 1e-12 of 1 come with a classed warning

**Status:** done (2026-10-18, PR #182 https://github.com/jmgirard/intraclass/pull/182)

**Goal:** A glmmTMB or lme4 Monte-Carlo or bootstrap interval whose lower limit lies within 1e-12 of 1 is still reported, and `icc()` tells the user, through a classed warning, that data with almost no residual variation can give such an interval.

**Outcome:** `warn_unresolved_interval()` (`R/ci-montecarlo.R`, bound `unresolved_interval_bound <- 1e-12`) runs after `refuse_zero_width()` in `mc_ci()` and `bootstrap_ci()`. On a glmmTMB or lme4 fit, when any returned interval's lower limit is finite and above 1 − 1e-12, it signals one `intraclass_unresolved_interval` warning (a subclass of `intraclass_warning`) and changes no interval. It takes no `hint`, and the hint trial runs muffle warnings, so it fires at most once per `icc()` call. Equal limits still abort first. Plan probes: lme4 on near-flat and exactly additive data puts lower limits 1e-14 to 1e-13 below 1, and so does data with rater noise 1e-7, whose intervals are correct (SEARLE exact-F agrees). So no width or fit statistic separates them, and the user chose a warning over a refusal. The bound reads every row, so average-rater rows warn at larger noise (D-055). New `tests/testthat/test-unresolved-interval.R`: stub tests on both sides of the bound (1e-13, 5e-13, 2.5e-12), the count, lavaan and equal-limits controls, an `icc()` grid on near-flat, additive and noise-1e-7 frames, and a noise-1e-5 control on both engines. NEWS, `?icc` (`ci_method`), DESIGN.md Monte-Carlo and Bootstrap rows, and an `out` row in `data-raw/mpl-doc-claims.tsv` for the new `?icc` sentence.

**Decisions:** D-054 (plan commit), D-055 (review: narrows D-054's sentence on where the bound lies to single-rater rows). The plan gate chose a warning over a refusal below a width, and the bound inside the reducers over one in `icc()`; each falsifier is in the work log in git.
