(a) The fixture test's two `all()` assertions are vacuously true on zero rows, and its anti-vacuity guard checks the total row count where AC1's requirement is per-arm, so a skewed regeneration passes (review F3/F4). (c) The fixture's `seed` column is the data seed while the reducer seed and engine live only in the prose header (F11). Promote on the next regeneration of that fixture or the next change to that script — the guards bite only under a future edit. Lineage: M104 review.
