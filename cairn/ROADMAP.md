- **Stale checkpoints can silently feed the M111 sweep the wrong rows** — `run_cell()` in `data-raw/m111-fallback-sweep.R` short-circuits on `data-raw/m111-fallback-checkpoints/cell-NN.rds` keyed on the cell **id alone**, and M112's `assert_sweep_results()` validates shape and id order but not cell parameters. So a re-run after editing `build_cells()` (reordered `dist` levels, a changed `rho` set) serves every cell from the old grid and passes every guard; a re-run intended to exercise M112's new status-based abort classification serves rows computed under the old finiteness-based flag with no error. Neither is introduced by M112 (the cache line is unchanged since M111) and neither touches the committed fixture, which is why both scored 30 at the M112 review. Fix shape: assert `rho`/`k`/`n`/`dist` against the cell spec alongside the id, or key the checkpoint on the cell's parameters. Promote on the next re-run or regeneration of the M111 sweep — it bites only then. Lineage: M112 review H-1/M-4.
- Burch's near-zero-MSA interval is **computed from floating-point roundoff** — `burch_kappa_hat()` standardizes by `sqrt(MSA)`, so at an MSA that is tiny but not exactly 0 both numerator and denominator are accumulated summation error. Measured at the M105 review (2026-08-05) on the committed 8×4 fixture cell: MSA = 3.52e-33, every subject-mean deviation exactly −2.78e-17 against `sqrt(MSA)` = 5.93e-17, ratio −0.468, `kappa_hat` = −1.7395, interval [−0.1865, 0.2974] at `unit = "single"`. **Pre-existing, not introduced by M105** — the same cell returned the same interval before it — which is why M105's guard (`identical(MSA, 0)`) leaves it alone and M105 AC3 certifies it as a valid finite result. Promote on a user reporting such an interval, or when the M105 re-cut decides the certification should not stand. Lineage: M105 review F1.

(c) The fixture's `seed` column is the data seed while the reducer seed and engine live only in the prose header (F11).
