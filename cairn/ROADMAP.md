A running-max envelope (only ever widens) or a monotone smoother would remove the dips — **chosen against at the M91 plan gate (2026-07-24)** for traceability: the shipped numbers would no longer be the ones M90's coverage sweep measured. **The first probe has now run and found no cost:** M91's three off-node cells (D1–D3, incl. Promote on a cell that falls below its floor, not on the non-monotonicity itself; per-cell figures are in § M92 of `cairn/references/mpl-twoway-random-comparison.md`. Lineage: M88 table → M90 tables → M91 plan gate → M91 T2 → M92 T7.
