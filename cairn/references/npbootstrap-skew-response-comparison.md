## What is being decided

D-027 judged three interval methods against a 0.93 per-cell floor on this grid
and recorded no replacement: `searle` and `burch` both no-GO, the `mc` incumbent
`warn`. `npbootstrap` — the ukoumunne2003 transformed bootstrap-t shipped at M75
— was never run on it. M121 measures that fourth leg on the same 64 cells and
records its disposition, so the repo can say for a fourth method what D-027
established for three.

The disposition is a recommendation to the maintainer gate and nothing more. The
default-method contract is D-001-fenced and is not traded here; a `replace-GO`
would commission a follow-on milestone, never a change in this one.

## Pre-registered disposition rule — frozen 2026-08-15, before any harness code or derived table (GP5)

**Known priors (authored with these in hand).** Three bodies of evidence about
this leg and this grid already exist, and the rule below was written knowing all
of them:

- **`npbootstrap` on the narrower M76 grid** (16 cells: ρ ∈ {0.05, 0.10},
  k ∈ {10, 30, 50}, n ∈ {2, 5}, gaussian and t5, n_rep = 2000): unconditional
  coverage 0.9245–0.9495 with **zero aborts at every cell**, dipping below 0.93
  at exactly one — 0.9245 at (ρ = 0.10, k = 10, n = 2, t5). That grid carries no
  chisq1 and no ρ above 0.10, so it says nothing about the skewed high-ρ corner
  where the other three legs fail worst.
- **The published anchors** (ukoumunne2003 Table I, gaussian, ρ = 0.05, n = 10):
  0.938 / 0.944 / 0.9395 at k = 10 / 30 / 50 — the reducer-level oracle AC2
  re-runs as a precondition, not evidence about the swept domain.
- **The three incumbents on this exact grid** (M113 table, D-027): below the 0.93
  `coverage_uncond` floor at 21/64 cells (`searle`), 16/64 (`burch`) and 49/64
  (`mc`; 36/64 on `coverage_nonabort`), worst cells 0.674 / 0.6655 / 0.3935.

What is genuinely blind is the whole of `npbootstrap`'s 64-cell table: no cell of
this grid has ever been run with that method.

**Tag vocabulary for the verdict (filled at T6).** `replace-GO` | `no-GO`,
reusing D-027's S1 vocabulary unchanged so the fourth leg is read against the
same words as the first three, and so D-027's reopening condition — "a method
demonstrating every-cell floor coverage over this grid" — reads directly off the
tag. `replace-GO` means the floor is cleared at every cell and the replacement
question is reopened for the maintainer; it is never itself a default change.

| # | Rule (binding unless marked) | Source of the threshold |
|---|---|---|
| N1 | **Floor bar for `npbootstrap`.** The leg is `replace-GO` iff its per-cell **`coverage_uncond`** — the unconditional coverage column of `data-raw/m121-npbootstrap-skew-coverage.tsv`, over all 2000 replicates of a cell including those where `icc()` signalled a classed `intraclass_*` condition — is ≥ 0.93 at every one of the 64 cells; otherwise `no-GO`. Reported alongside, descriptive and never binding: the failing cells partitioned by abort rate at 0.1, with `coverage_nonabort` for each part; the worst cell with its (ρ, k, n, dist); and the median width ratio against the `mc` leg. | floor: M76 C2 (ukoumunne2003 worst tabulated 0.931; burch2011 §5 "just under 0.93"); every-cell form and the unconditional column: D-027 S1 |

**Why the unconditional column.** D-027 judged `searle` and `burch` on
`coverage_uncond` and this leg is being compared to them, so the column is
inherited rather than chosen. The abort-rate split is reported because D-026
already adjudicated the abort event as informative: a failing cell above the 0.1
abort boundary is that adjudicated selection effect, not a fresh finding, and the
split is what lets a reader tell the two apart without moving the bar.

**Aggregation (frozen).** N1 yields exactly one tag, read directly off the
derived table. Whether D-027's S1 reopening condition is met follows from that
tag and is stated explicitly in the same D-entry. The verdict — one tag plus the
reopening statement — is recorded as one D-entry, and no bracketed claim token is
minted for it.
